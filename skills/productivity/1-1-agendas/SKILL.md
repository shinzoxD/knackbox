# 1:1 Agendas

1:1s are for the report’s topics first: growth, blockers, feedback, and
relationship — not a status meeting that should be a doc.

## Structure (30–45 min)

```markdown
# 1:1 — <names> — <date>

## Their topics (first)
- …

## Your topics
- …

## Feedback (both directions)
- …

## Career / growth
- …

## Action items
- …
```

## Rules

1. Status updates → written async unless blocking.
2. Psychological safety: hard topics with care, not ambush HR processes you’re not authorized to run.
3. Recurring themes get tracked actions.
4. Prep questions beat free-form silence.
5. Notes private by default; share actions explicitly.
6. Canceling repeatedly is a signal — surface it.

## Edge cases

- **New hire 1:1s:** more context and expectations.
- **Skip-levels:** no undermining direct manager; gather themes.
- **Performance concerns:** follow company process; document facts.
