# Internal Comms

Internal messages compete with noise. Lead with what changes for the reader,
when, and what they should do.

## Structure

```markdown
Subject / Slack title:

Opening (context in 1–2 lines)

What is changing
- …

Why (brief)

What you need to do
- …

Timeline
…

FAQ
…

Owner / questions channel
```

## Rules

1. No surprise bury: hard news early, humanly.
2. Do not invent legal/HR policy; use provided facts.
3. Separate FYI from action-required.
4. Tone: respectful, specific, jargon-light.
5. Reorgs: reporting lines and customer impact; avoid gossip framing.
6. Offer short Slack + longer email variants when useful.

## Edge cases

- **Bad news:** acknowledge impact; concrete support paths if given.
- **Multi-audience:** eng vs company-wide versions.
- **Rumors:** stick to confirmed statements only.
